How to read this study

  • Method: for each device the clock starts at its installation date (assignedToVehicleAt). The "event / failure" is the device going silent for more than 14 days; time-to-failure is the span from install to its last signal.
  • Censoring: devices that are still transmitting have not failed yet; they enter as censored observations up to today — this is what makes it a true survival analysis rather than a plain failure count.
  • Kaplan–Meier estimates S(t) = the probability a device is still transmitting t days after install, corrected for censoring.
  • Caveat: "silence" is not necessarily a hardware fault — it can be a parked vehicle, a suspended SIM, or an uninstalled unit. The result measures transmission continuity, a practical signal for proactive maintenance.
